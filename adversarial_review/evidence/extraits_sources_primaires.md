## Règlement (UE) 2019/943, tel que modifié par le règlement (UE) 2024/1747 — EUR-Lex CELEX 32024R1747

- Art. 19e(1)(a) : « be consistent with the European resource adequacy assessment and national resource adequacy assessments conducted pursuant to Articles 23 and 24 ».
- Art. 19e(2)(a) : « evaluate the different types of flexibility needs, at least on a seasonal, daily and hourly basis, to integrate electricity generated from renewable sources in the electricity system, inter alia, different assumptions in respect to electricity market prices, generation and demand ».
- Art. 19e(4)(b)(i) : « all available sources of flexibility in a cost-efficient manner in the different timeframes, including in other Member States ».
- Art. 19e(7)(b) : ACER shall assess « the estimated flexibility needs in the electricity system at Union level and its projected economically available potential for a period of the next 5 to 10 years taking into account the national reports ».
- Art. 19f : « each Member State shall define, on the basis of that report, an indicative national objective for non-fossil flexibility, including the respective specific contributions of both demand response and energy storage to that objective ».

## Méthodologie ERAA révisée — Décision ACER 04/2026, annexe Ia

- Art. 4(1)(g) : « Unless the modelling framework allows for a proper characterisation of unforeseen imbalances, the ED shall rely on a "perfect foresight" principle: under this assumption, forecast errors of wind, solar, hydro generation, of planned outages as well as of demand are ignored in the ED. Additionally, unplanned outages are assumed to be known in advance with the perfect foresight principle. »
- Art. 4(1)(h) : « The MTU shall be smaller than or equal to an hour. »
- Climat : « the central reference scenarios shall either i. rely on a best forecast of future climate projections; ii. weight climate years to reflect their likelihood of occurrence […]; or iii. rely at most on the 30 most recent historical climatic years included in the PECD. »
- Monte-Carlo : « The number of MC sample years shall ensure convergence of the results » ; « The convergence of the Monte Carlo method shall be assessed by the coefficient of variation (α) of the ENS » ; à publier : « The number of analysed Monte Carlo years N; The value of α as a function of N. »
- Hydraulique, art. 4(5)(a) : « some of which include an ex-ante optimisation phase with a coarser time resolution than the timestep of the ED model (e.g. weekly or even seasonal decomposition […]) » ; « The hydro optimisation shall reflect: i. the expected operational principles applied for each target year by market participants which own and operate hydro storage ».
- EVA : viabilité fondée sur les revenus, ou « By way of simplification, ENTSO-E may instead apply an approach based on the minimisation of overall system costs ».
- EVA, risque : « the reaction of investors in EVA towards price spikes shall be mitigated » ; « The default for this complementary approach shall be a partial discounting of Monte Carlo sample years with the highest revenues. »

## ACER, Décision 07/2025 (ERAA 2024), annexe III technique

- « As in previous editions of ERAA, ERAA 2024 formulates the EVA as an optimisation problem that minimises total (fixed and operating) system costs. »
- « Notable differences can be observed, with average increases ranging from 1.7h to 4h of LOLE depending on the target year considered. These differences highlight that perceived adequacy risks are not the same between the two modules, hence that scarcity situations and pricing are not accurately reflected in the EVA, distorting investment signals. »
- « The most important of these simplifications are the reduction of the modelled weather scenarios from 36 in the ED to 3 in the EVA […] »

## HMMCP SDAC — Décision ACER 01/2023, annexe 1 (et Décision 02/2026)

- « The reference harmonised maximum clearing price for SDAC shall be +4000 EUR/MWh. »
- « the harmonised maximum clearing price for SDAC shall be increased by 500 EUR/MWh in the event that the clearing price, in at least one bidding zone, exceeds a value of 70 percent of the harmonised maximum clearing price for SDAC in at least 2 market time units in at least 2 different days within 30 rolling days from the first price spike » ; « the transition period shall be set to 28 days following the completion of the event ».
- La Décision 02/2026 porte sur les conditions d'exclusion (liquidité, représentativité des pics) ; les paramètres ci-dessus ne sont pas modifiés.
